Job Title:
HR Recruiter

About the Job:
We are looking for an HR Recruiter to own the end-to-end hiring process for our
growing company. You will partner with hiring managers to source, screen, and
hire great people across technical and non-technical roles.

Responsibilities:
- Source and screen candidates across multiple channels.
- Conduct interviews and coordinate the interview process.
- Write and post job descriptions on job boards and social media.
- Manage candidate pipelines in the applicant tracking system.
- Partner with hiring managers to build strong talent pipelines.

Required Qualifications:
- 3+ years of experience in recruitment or talent acquisition.
- Strong interviewing, communication, and organizational skills.
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Psychology, Business, or a related field.

Required Skills:
- Recruitment
- Candidate screening
- Interviewing
- Talent acquisition
- Job posting
- Applicant tracking systems
- HR administration
- Communication

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience sourcing technical talent.
- Familiarity with diversity hiring best practices.
- Experience using LinkedIn Recruiter and Boolean search.

Experience Requirements:
- 3+ years of professional recruitment experience.

Education Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree or higher in a relevant field.
