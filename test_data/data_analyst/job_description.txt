Job Title:
Data Analyst

About the Job:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to turn raw data into actionable insights for
product and business teams. You will build reports, dashboards, and analyses
that guide decisions across the company.

Responsibilities:
- Write SQL queries to extract and clean data from warehouse tables.
- Build dashboards in Power BI or Tableau for business stakeholders.
- Analyze trends and present findings with clear data visualizations.
- Maintain Excel models and automate recurring analysis with Python.
- Collaborate with data engineers to improve data quality.

Required Qualifications:
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis or a related role.
- Strong command of SQL and Python for data analysis.
- Bachelor's degree in a quantitative or related field.

Required Skills:
- SQL
- Python
- Pandas
- Excel
- Power BI
- Statistics
- Data visualization
- Data analysis

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with Tableau or Looker.
- Familiarity with A/B testing and experimentation.
- Knowledge of basic statistics and hypothesis testing.

Experience Requirements:
- 2+ years of professional data analysis experience.

Education Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree or higher in a quantitative field.
