Job Title:
UI/UX Designer

About the Job:
We are looking for a UI/UX Designer to shape intuitive, delightful product
experiences. You will work with product managers to research user needs, design
interfaces, and validate solutions before they are built.

Responsibilities:
- Conduct user research and usability testing to inform design decisions.
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and user flows for new features.
- Maintain and extend the product design system.
- Partner with product and engineering to hand off polished designs.
- Iterate on designs based on user feedback and product analytics.

Required Qualifications:
- 2+ years of experience in product or interface design.
- A portfolio demonstrating interface design and prototyping work.
- Bachelor's degree in Design, Human-Computer Interaction, or a related field.

Required Skills:
- Figma
- User interface design
- User experience research
- Wireframing
- Prototyping
- Design systems
- User flows
- Usability testing

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience planning and moderating user interviews.
- Familiarity with accessibility best practices.
- Experience working in agile product teams.

Experience Requirements:
- 2+ years of professional design experience.

Education Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree or higher in a relevant field.
